How this works

What's included

Every morning Beangrid reads the public product catalogue of each roaster's Shopify store and keeps only roasted coffee beans. Gear, merch, syrups, tea, subscriptions, gift cards, pods, instant and green coffee are filtered out. Grind options of the same bag are merged into one row.

How deals are scored

Price drop: at least 5% below the coffee's typical price over the past 30 days. On sale: the roaster lists a higher "compare at" price (markdowns running 60+ days count as the regular price). Value: how its price per kilo ranks against every other in-stock coffee in the same bag size. The deal score blends discount (60%) and value (40%).

Questionable data

Bag sizes come from the label ("340g", "5 lb") rather than Shopify's shipping weight, which is often wrong. Rows are hidden by default when the size can't be found, or the price per kilo is implausible or far below the roaster's norm. Those are almost always typos in the store. Untick Hide questionable data to see them.
